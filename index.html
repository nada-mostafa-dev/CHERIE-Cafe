OUR HERITAGE

A Little Love in Every Bite

Born from a deep reverence for classical Parisian salon traditions and contemporary café hospitality, CHÉRIE was created as a sanctuary for the senses. Every creation begins with deliberate intention: small-batch baking, pristine temperature control, and uncompromised culinary ethics.

Pure Ingredients

Normandy butter, grand cru Belgian cacao, and pesticide-free berries harvested daily.

Artisanal Craft

Zero shortcuts. Baked fresh in small batches every morning at 5:00 AM.

Welcoming Haven

Designed for slow conversations, laughter, notebook scribbles, and timeless company.
